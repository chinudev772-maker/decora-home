Our Story

Designed for calm, made to last

DECORA HOME began with a simple idea: a home should feel restful the moment you walk in. We work with small workshops and craftspeople to make pieces in honest materials such as solid wood, clay, linen and brass.

Every piece in the collection is chosen to sit well with the others, so a room comes together easily, one piece at a time.

  • Natural, long-lasting materials
  • Made in small batches by skilled craftspeople
  • Free shipping on orders above ₹999
